THE DESERT

Tabernas, Almería: canyons, red rock and the sunsets they filmed westerns in. Thirty minutes from Almería city, a world away from everything else.

  • Shuttle Free buses every 30 minutes from Almería airport and train station.
  • Camping Shaded camp with showers, lockers and 24h water points. Glamping tents for VIP.
  • Cashless Load your wristband once, tap to pay everywhere.
  • Leave no trace Reusable cups, refill stations and a crew that leaves the desert spotless.

GOOD TO KNOW

What age do I need to be?

Emberland is 18+. Bring a valid ID or passport, your name must match your ticket.

Can I change the name on my ticket?

Yes, free of charge until 1 July 2027 from your ticket account.

How hot does it get?

Days reach 35°C, nights cool to around 20°C. Shade, water points and misting zones are everywhere.

Is it accessible?

We have accessible viewing platforms, camping, toilets and shuttles. Write to us and we’ll plan your visit together.
